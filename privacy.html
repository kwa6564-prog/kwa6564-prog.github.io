Privacy Policy - KWA TikTok Manager

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KWA TikTok Manager handles data.

  1. Data collected: The App accesses TikTok account data strictly necessary to authenticate and publish video content on behalf of the connected account, as permitted by the scopes granted during TikTok login.
  2. Use of data: Data is used only to operate the App's core function - creating and publishing video posts to the connected TikTok account. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.
  3. Storage: Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as needed. Users may revoke access at any time through their TikTok account settings.
  4. Third parties: The App does not share user data with third parties except as required to operate the integration with TikTok's API.
  5. Contact: For privacy questions, contact mertozelci37@icloud.com.
